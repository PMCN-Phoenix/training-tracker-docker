## 二、实际抓到摘要的文献（可在报告中引用）

1. **Schoenfeld BJ et al. 2017** — Strength and hypertrophy adaptations between low- vs. high-load resistance training: a systematic review and meta-analysis. *J Strength Cond Res.* 31(12):3508-3523. PMID: 28834797.
   - 摘要要点：21 RCT；高负荷 1RM 力量增益 > 低负荷；增肌无显著差异。
2. **Signorile JF et al. 2017** — Differences in Muscle Activation and Kinematics Between Cable-Based and Selectorized Weight Training. *J Strength Cond Res.* 31(2):313-322. PMID: 28129277.
   - 摘要要点：n=15；缆绳弯举里胸大肌/前三角肌激活更高；**二头肌本身无显著差异**。
3. **Parpa K et al. 2025** — An Exploratory Study of Biceps Brachii Electromyographic Activity During Traditional Dumbbell Versus Bayesian Cable Curls. *Muscles.* 4(4):45. PMID: 41133617.
   - 摘要要点：探索性，未给方向性结论。
4. **Tilp M et al. 2022** — Electromyographic and Systemic Physiological Thresholds in Single-Joint Elbow Flexion Movements. *Int J Sports Physiol Perform.* 17(2):241-248. PMID: 34611058.
   - 摘要要点：n=18；二头肌长短头 EMG 拐点在 ~32% 和 ~65% 最大负荷。
5. **Cowley N et al. 2026** — Advanced RT Prescription Methods Bayesian Network Meta-analysis. *Sports Med.* 56(8):1955-1977. PMID: 41951916.
   - 只抓到标题，未精读全文。
6. **Schoenfeld BJ et al. 2015** — Effect of repetition duration during resistance training on muscle hypertrophy. *Sports Med.* 45(4):577-585. PMID: 25601394.
   - 只抓到标题。
7. 国内临床指南 2 份（肌少症基层 2025、心衰康复共识）——作为"5%–10% 周递增"和"次数达标升档"的出处。

## 四、工具受限说明

- `general_search` 在本会话中被限流（连续 3 个 query 都返回 "This search was restricted. No results were returned."），未能使用。
- `seed_medical_search` 只覆盖中文医学教材/共识，对英文运动科学 RCT/EMG 研究覆盖差。
- `web_fetch` 在后期连续返回 "Function not found"，未能继续抓取 Cowley 2026 / Schoenfeld 2015 全文摘要，也未能补搜 hammer/preacher/incline EMG。
- 因此本报告的"动作 EMG 对比表"只保留了 3 条实际抓到摘要的研究，其余全部标"未找到直接文献"。
