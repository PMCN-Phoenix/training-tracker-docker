## 二、关键摘要摘录（按主题分组，保留英文原文片段）

### 主题 1：意象时皮质脊髓兴奋性（Q1 神经机制）

**Hashimoto & Rothwell 1999, Exp Brain Res**
> "On average, the evoked EMG responses were larger in the flexor muscle during the phase of imagined flexion than during extension… The EMG remained relaxed in all muscles… The H-reflex in the flexor muscle… showed no change… motor imagery can have dynamic effects on the excitability of motor cortex similar to those seen during actual motor performance."

**Morozova et al. 2024, Sci Rep**
> "MEPs increased during both kMI and TI, with a stronger increase for kMI… kMI involves a mental enactment of kinesthetic sensations resulting from voluntary movements (muscle contraction, joint position, tendons tension, etc.)"

**Decety et al. 1993, J Physiol**
> "During mental simulation, both heart and ventilatory rate increased immediately after mental exercise was begun. This increase was proportional to the amount of simulated exercise… NMR spectra were unchanged… mental simulation of action can activate heart and respiration control mechanisms."

**Kilteni et al. 2018, Nat Commun（核心证据）**
> "Our results demonstrate that imagined self-touch is attenuated just as real self-touch is and that the imagery-induced attenuation follows the same spatiotemporal principles as does the attenuation elicited by overt movements. We conclude that motor imagery recruits the forward models to predict the sensory consequences of imagined movements."

**Jeannerod 1995, Neuropsychologia**
> "Motor images are endowed with the same properties as those of the corresponding motor representations… many neural mechanisms are activated during motor imagery, as revealed by a sharp increase in tendinous reflexes in the limb imagined to move, and by vegetative changes which correlate with the level of mental effort."

### 主题 2：筋膜链学术地位（诚实分层）

**Wilke et al. 2016, Arch Phys Med Rehabil**
> "The studies reviewed suggest strong evidence for the existence of 3 myofascial meridians: the superficial back line (all 3 transitions verified, 14 studies), the back functional line (all 3, 8 studies) and the front functional line (both, 6 studies). Moderate-to-strong evidence for parts of the spiral line (5/9) and lateral line (2/5). **No evidence exists for the superficial front line (no verified transition, 7 studies).**"

**Krause et al. 2016, J Anatomy**
> "Concerning the SBL and BFL, there is moderate evidence for force transfer… One study yields moderate evidence for a slight, but not significant force transfer at one transition in the FFL… different methods of force application and measurement hinder the comparability of results."

**Maas & Sandercock 2010, J Biomed Biotechnol（反证关键）**
> "In a reduced preparation, involving supraphysiological muscle conditions, it is shown that connective tissues surrounding muscles are capable of transmitting substantial force. **In more physiologically relevant conditions of intact muscles, however, it appears that the role of this myofascial pathway is small.**"

**Nichols 2018, J Neurophysiol（GTO 力反馈）**
> "Force feedback is widely distributed across the muscles of a limb… autogenic, excitatory force feedback contributes to propulsion and weight support… coexistent inhibitory force feedback, together with length feedback, functions to manage interjoint coordination."

**Ito et al. 1998, J Appl Physiol（等长收缩中腱被拉长——核心力学证据）**
> "Fascicle length shortened from 90±7 to 76±7 mm, pennation angle increased… tendon elongation increased up to 15±2 mm with graded force development… tendon stiffness increased from 10 N/mm at 0–20 N to 32 N/mm at 240–260 N… in isometric contractions of a human muscle, mechanical work… is absorbed by the tendinous tissue."

### 主题 3：意象练力量元分析

**Paravlic et al. 2018, Sports Med**
> "MI practice moderately improved MVS. When compared to conventional PP, effects were of small benefit in favour of PP… meta-regression: (a) training period of 4 weeks, (b) frequency of three times per week, (c) two to three sets per single session, (d) 25 repetitions per set, and (e) single session duration of 15 min… MI practice increases MVS, but less than PP."

**Liu et al. 2022, Front Psychol**
> "MIT demonstrated significant benefit on enhancing muscle strength… ES 1.10, 95% CI 0.89–1.30, favoring MIT, but was inferior to physical training (PT), ES 0.38… MIT was more effective for older adults (ES 2.17) than young adults (ES 0.95), and for small finger muscles (ES 1.64) than large upper extremity muscles (ES 0.86)."

**Slimani et al.（综述）**
> "Advantageous effects of internal imagery (range from 2.6 to 136.3%) for strength performance compared with external imagery (range from 4.8 to 23.2%)… imagining 'lifting a heavy object' resulted in more EMG activity compared with imagining 'lifting a lighter object'."

**Yao et al. 2023, Front Psychol（双侧迁移）**
> "Internal MIT (IMIT) was more effective (ES=2.17) than external MIT (EMIT) (ES=0.95), and mixed-task (ES=1.68) was more effective than mirror-task (ES=0.46) and normal-task (ES=0.56)."

### 主题 4：注意焦点（Wulf）

**Wulf & Lewthwaite 2016, OPTIMAL**
> "External focus of attention on the intended movement effect… motivational and attentional factors contribute to performance and learning by strengthening the coupling of goals to actions."

**注意**：在力量/意象领域，内部动觉意象反而更强（见主题 3），与 Wulf 经典结论形成表面冲突——调和解释已在术语映射文件 §9.3。

### 主题 5：睡眠巩固

**Di Rienzo et al. 2023, Cerebral Cortex**
> "Behavioral analyses revealed that overnight consolidation of motor learning through motor imagery outperformed wake-related consolidation… We provide the first evidence of parallel brain plasticity underlying behavioral changes associated with sleep-dependent consolidation of motor skill learning through motor imagery and physical practice."

**Debarnot et al. 2011**
> "A daytime nap after imagery practice improved motor performance and, therefore, facilitated motor memory consolidation, as compared with spending a similar time interval in the wake state."

### 主题 6：个体化/反证

**Parkin et al. 2012, Mindfulness（身体扫描反证）**
> "No significant differences in change in cardiac perception accuracy… Cardiac perception accuracy and coherence did not improve, but participants became significantly more confident… mindfulness practice does not simply work by improving cardiac perception accuracy but does increase confidence."

**Treves et al. 2019, Sci Rep（元分析）**
> "Across 15 studies (17 independent samples), a small effect was found linking mindfulness with greater body awareness accuracy (g=0.21 [0.08, 0.34], N=879)."

**Dupont et al. 2024, Brain Comms（aphantasia）**
> "Corticospinal excitability is facilitated by kinaesthetic imagery and action observation in typical participants… these facilitation effects were not found in participants reporting aphantasia."

**Tassignon et al. 2021, Front Psychol（DL 元分析）**
> "Overall small effect size of 0.26 in the acquisition phase… medium effect size of 0.61 in the retention phase… large heterogeneity… high risk of bias… inferences about the effectiveness of DL would be premature."

**Hossner et al. 2015, Hum Mov Sci（DL 反证）**
> "In both experiments, the advantage of DL over repetitive learning was not statistically significant."

**Van Rossum 1990（图式反证）**
> "About half of the experiments were factually not addressing the variability prediction… only limited support favouring the prediction could be obtained… cannot rest upon consistent supportive evidence, neither with adult nor with child subjects."
