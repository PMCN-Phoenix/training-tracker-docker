## 二、核心论点摘录（带 URL + 时间戳）

### 2.1 科学综述：SSC 与周期化（N3tcL_UJkZI）

URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N3tcL_UJkZI

> **[00:30 起]** "About half of the force involved in jumping upward is from the elastic in the neuromuscular component... the stretch shortening cycle contributes about half of the force to vertical jump. The other half comes from active muscular components."

> **[03:00]** "A better system for most athletes: 1 month hypertrophy → 1 month strength → 1 month strength/power → 1 month power and plyometric specific training (peak)."

> **[06:00]** "Plyometric training can increase vertical jump by about 5–9% in the average person."

> **[07:00]** "Beginner: 50–100 total jumps per workout, 1–3×/week. Advanced: 120–140 foot contacts per session, 2–4×/week."

> **[10:00]** "Over many studies, the countermovement jump had the greatest effect size... Half squats are really effective in U17 basketball players because that's very joint-angle specific to the vertical jump movement pattern."

**关键数据**：
- 男性平均 VJ 15–22 inch（38–56cm）
- 8 周研究：week 1 = 50 jumps → week 8 = 170 jumps + 8×10 depth jumps，平均提升 5%

### 2.2 12 周周期模板（1CFAZfxEkoQ）

URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1CFAZfxEkoQ

> **[01:30] Phase 1 (4 weeks)**：hypertrophy + low plyo。Loaded jumps 持 10–15% BW 哑铃，4×5，quarter squat 幅度。Front squat / back squat / leg press 全幅度。

> **[04:00] Phase 2 (4 weeks)**：ramp plyometric + strength。Reps 降到 6–8，加 single-leg lateral barrier hop、depth jump。

> **[07:00] Phase 3 (4 weeks)**：most specific to VJ。Unload / band-assisted jumps，最短 ground contact，最少重量，最 tax 神经。

> **[09:00] 周结构**：Mon/Thu = primary lower body power days；Tue = conditioning；Wed = upper body；Fri = mobility；Sat = full body。

> **[11:00]** "Expect 1–2 inch (2.5–5cm) vertical jump improvement each month."

### 2.3 3 大错误（oxCbu1A4WtQ）—— 反面教材

URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxCbu1A4WtQ

> **[03:00] Mistake 1: Too much volume.** Pro 球员训练 session 只有 ~80 jumps，全部 strategic。Ralph Mann："**one training session should have less than 3 minutes of total high-CNS stress activity**."

> **[05:00] Mistake 2: Not enough rest between sets.** Plyometric 必须放在 session 最前面（CNS 新鲜时）。组间休息 3 分钟。CMJ 下降不超过 10% 才算合格。

> **[09:00] Mistake 3: Wrong load.** Alabama football 数据：peak power 在 **65–70% 1RM**（barbell squat）。Olympic lift 类 ~80% 1RM。Ballistic 类（med ball throw、loaded jump）20–40% BW 即可。

**关键原则**：根据运动员 deficit 调整——如果 bouncy 但不强 → 多练 force end；如果强但慢 → 多练 velocity end。

### 2.4 10 年扣将实战建议（1Qgf4_bphpo，Isaiah Rivera）

URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1Qgf4_bphpo

> **[00:30]** "Never go more than a week without jumping."（健康前提下）

> **[01:00]** "Sprint a couple times a week as fast as you can. Start with resisted/hill sprints, every 4 weeks shift to more upright running."

> **[02:30]** "Lift heavy a couple times per week. Every 4–6 weeks, change the exercise — try front squats, lunges, step-ups."

> **[05:00]** "If you want to jump high, get under 10% body fat. This is the lowest-hanging fruit."

> **[07:00]** "Alternate volume and intensity. Never high volume + high intensity in the same week."

> **[10:00]** "Don't force positions your body isn't built for — deep squatting is great, but if your hip anatomy doesn't allow it, you'll destroy your hips."

### 2.5 Jump Science 2.0 评测（5W3lKB59YUI）—— 反面案例

URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5W3lKB59YUI

> **[01:00] 分档逻辑**：
> - Level 1：不能 goblet squat 25kg 哑铃全幅度
> - Level 2：不能 squat 1.5×BW
> - Level 3+：按 squat 2×BW 和 relative vert（VJ/身高）分档

> **[02:00] 10 周结构**：3×3 周 phases + Week 7 rest。Weeks 1–3 depth drop（sticking landing，不跳）→ Weeks 4–6 depth jump → Weeks 8–10 sprint + bounding。

> **[03:00] 规则**：depth jump ground contact < 0.3s（自己看视频逐帧测）。

> **[04:00] 反面结果**：评测者 10 周后 running VJ 只到 66cm（比之前 Jump Bible 还低），standing VJ 58cm（与开始持平）。Weeks 8–9 standing VJ 反而下降。

**教训**：付费程序 ≠ 一定涨。个体差异、前期训练水平、动作选择都影响结果。不要盲目跟任何单一 program。

### 2.6 Garage Strength：SSC 与肌腱刚度（yAG9eO8CwHw）

URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yAG9eO8CwHw

> **[02:00]** "The stiffer the joint tendons at the bottom of the eccentric movement, the faster we can get out of the amortization phase... we need to increase our eccentric capability, which leads to more stiffness in the joints."

> 核心：弹跳不是练某块肌肉（不是 calf raises / leg curls 就能跳更高），而是练整个后链 + 肌腱刚度 + SSC。

### 2.7 Overtime Athletes 5 参数框架（Oswoi1RHB1U）

URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Oswoi1RHB1U

> **[01:00] 5 个参数按顺序**：
> 1. Mobilization（髋屈肌、踝背屈活动度）
> 2. Activation（臀肌激活）
> 3. Plyometrics（吸收力量 → 转化为跳跃）
> 4. Power（light load + explode，如 loaded squat jump）
> 5. Brute strength（triphasic：eccentric block → isometric block → concentric block）

## 四、对用户的可执行模板（直接照搬）

基于 The Movement System 12 周模板 + 学术剂量：

**当前定位**：1.20×BW，还没到 depth jump 准入线（1.5×BW）→ Phase 1 为主，不要上 depth jump。

**Phase 1（4–8 周）：力量打底 + 低强度 plyo**
- Mon：背蹲 5×5（渐进到 80% 1RM）+ front squat 3×8 + ankle hop 3×10
- Thu：背蹲 4×6 + RDL 3×8 + CMJ 3×5（max effort）
- 每周 total foot contacts：60–80（低强度）
- 目标：深蹲 1RM 从 96 → 110 kg（1.38×BW）

**Phase 2（4–8 周）：过 depth jump 准入线**
- Mon：背蹲 5×3（85–90%）+ hang high pull 4×5（60% hang clean 1RM）+ depth jump 3×5（30cm 箱）
- Thu：front squat 4×5 + CMJ 3×5 + hurdle hop 3×8
- 每周 total foot contacts：100–120
- 目标：深蹲 1RM → 120 kg（1.50×BW），CMJ → 65cm

**Phase 3（4–8 周）：专项转化**
- Mon：speed-strength（jump squat 30–45% 1RM）+ depth jump 4×5（45cm 箱）
- Thu：力量维持（背蹲 3×3 @ 80%）+ approach jump practice 5×3
- 每周 total foot contacts：120–150
- 目标：助跑 VJ → 80cm+
